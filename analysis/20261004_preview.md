### Matchup keys
1. KT offensive rebounding vs Samsung defensive rebounding. KT posted 13 and 19 OREB in its two open matches.
2. Bass usage and turnovers: KT's offense can become too Bass-heavy. Samsung should force Bass into crowded decisions rather than pure scoring possessions.
3. Two-foreign phase: neither pair has been consistently positive; KT has the better single-game ceiling, Samsung has not yet shown a winning shared stint.
4. Kanter playmaking: his 14/19/10 line vs DB shows Samsung can run offense through him, not only post him.
5. Perimeter variance: Samsung has enough shooters to punish KT if KT repeats the perimeter breakdown that allowed Sono 20 threes.
6. Guard control: Kang Sung-wook/Oxzón vs Han Ho-bin/Gu Tang ball security and entry quality may decide which foreign pair receives cleaner touches.

### Working prediction
- KT 55%, Samsung 45%.
- Working score: KT 86-82 Samsung.
- Moderate-to-low confidence. KT's home court, offensive rebounding and Bass's creation give a small edge, but Samsung has a real upset path if Kanter controls the paint and KT's perimeter defense breaks down.
- Same-day swing factors: confirmed starters, any late injury news, and whether Samsung starts Kanter or McCullough as the first foreign option.

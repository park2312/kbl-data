Rationale:

2 DB — strongest preseason/open-match evidence plus 95-86 regular opener. Ellenson+Hammonds creates unusual two-big spacing/physicality, Albano support structure improved, domestic shooting depth showed vs Sono.
3 JKG — 2-0 and best ball-security/connected-offense signal so far. Opener vs Mobis 26 AST/6 TO; Mooney+Thomas pairing has already won a major two-foreign matchup. Current concern is rebounding/OREB and whether scoring depth holds.
4 Sono — 1-1, but loss came on true 24h B2B at DB. O'Bryant/James/Quiambao/Lee Jung-hyun/Lee Jae-do gives multiple creation paths and modern spacing. Concerns: transition defense under fatigue, O'Bryant double-team counters, volatile 3P dependence.
5 KCC — huge variance. 68-103 loss to LG followed by 97-87 win at Mobis. Long+King combined 57 in Game 2 and Heo Hoon 17/13 AST. Ceiling is top-3; floor is lower because transition defense, depth and wing-size losses remain concerns. Heo Woong has not shot normally yet.
6 SK — no regular-season sample. Open matches lost 86-99 Sono and 85-103 DB with injuries to Ahn Young-jun/Kim Nak-hyun/Alvin Tolentino. Warney remains elite (29/13/5 vs DB), Hicks pairing has theoretical defensive/rebounding value. Rank is highest-uncertainty slot and can move quickly once Oct 18 regular season begins.
7 Samsung — 81-76 road win at KT. Kanter+McCullough two-foreign ceiling looked better than preseason prior; Han Ho-bin 8 AST/1 TO. Concern: lost rebounding 34-44 and allowed 22 OREB; need repeatability beyond one game.
8 Mobis — 0-2 but talent exceeds record. Lost by 3 to JKG, then 10 to KCC. Prim production is strong, but Bazley was benched for entire second half vs KCC after playing all Q2; Prim/Bazley/Lee Seung-hyun architecture is unsettled. Domestic scoring and Park Moo-bin shot threat are concerns.
9 KT — 0-1. Bass 35/11 but team only 76; coach acknowledged over-searching for Bass and failure to execute pre-Bass actions. 22 OREB is a real strength. Jeon 1/9 should not yet be treated as structural shooting decline. Kim Sun-hyung long-term absence and Ha Yoon-gi conditioning lower early-season floor.
10 Gas — 0-2, though both games competitive (73-82 Sono, 80-86 JKG). Ra is productive and Aluma has ceiling, but Aluma foul trouble has disrupted both-game structure, Bellangel has not yet supplied expected scoring, and JKG outrebounded them 40-28. This is not a hopeless last-place team; 8-10 gap is small.

## Bass defense and KT offensive adjustment
Viewer observation: Yang Hong-seok and Tamayo alternated on Bass.

LG did send help when Bass entered the paint and was willing to concede selected perimeter shots, especially Moon Jung-hyun.
KT punished the scheme:

This is the major structural improvement. Bass scored 12 fewer points but KT scored 21 more (76 -> 97).
Moon Jung-hyun hit two early threes; LG's planned concession failed.
KT placed shooters/secondary pieces around Bass and converted kickouts.
This directly answers the previous concern "Bass gets stats but team offense dies": against LG, Bass's gravity became team offense rather than replacing team offense.

## Tactical tension
### DB offensive pressure on Marei
Ellenson's stretch ability can pull a traditional paint anchor away from the rim.
Last season Oct 5 is the clearest example: Ellenson hit 4/7 from three and scored 23.
If Marei stays deep, Ellenson pick-and-pop / above-the-break 3 can punish.
If Marei steps high, Albano driving lanes and DB cuts/rolls can open.

### LG offensive pressure on Ellenson
Marei attacks the opposite weakness: physical paint play, offensive rebounding, screen/handoff hub play.
Oct 5: Marei had 9 OREB; LG won OREB 15-4 and second chances 15-3.
Mar 15: Marei 28/17/5/5 STL.
If Ellenson is pulled into repeated box-out/post/screen contact, DB loses some transition and offensive energy.

## Current preliminary matchup edge
- Pure scoring versatility: Ellenson.
- Rebounding/paint physicality: Marei clearly.
- Passing/hub impact: Marei, though Ellenson can pass.
- Spacing pressure: Ellenson clearly.
- Defensive anchoring: Marei clearly.
- Q1/Q4 one-foreign environment: slight Marei structural edge because he can attack Ellenson's defensive/rebounding workload, but Ellenson's shooting is the exact archetype that can distort Marei's preferred paint position.
- Q2/Q3: do NOT score as Ellenson vs Marei. Hammonds' defensive role changes the geometry; DB can protect Ellenson while keeping his offense.
